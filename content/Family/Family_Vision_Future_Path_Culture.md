## Our Family Vision

- **Core Values (Do's)**: Identify the essential principles and behaviors that guide your family's decisions and actions.
- **Our Family's Purpose**: Define the underlying reason for your family's existence. This could include your shared goals, aspirations, and what brings you together.
- **Why is it Important?**: Explain the significance of your family's purpose and how it impacts each member.
- **Against Our Values (Don'ts)**: Establish behaviors and actions that contradict your family's core values.
- **Action Plan**: Outline specific goals and initiatives to uphold your values and achieve your family's purpose.

## Our Family's Future

- **What Does Success Look Like for Each of Us?**: Describe each family member's personal vision of success.
- **Why is this Important?**: Reflect on why achieving this personal vision matters to each individual.
- **What Does Ultimate Success Look Like for Our Family?**: Define the long-term vision and ultimate goals for your family.
- **Why is this Important?**: Explain the importance of achieving this family vision.

## Our Family's Path

- **How Will We Spend Our Time?**: Determine how your family will allocate time for various activities, such as work, education, leisure, and personal growth.
- **How Will We Support Each Other?**: Identify ways your family will provide emotional, financial, and practical support to each member.
- **How Will We Make Decisions?**: Establish a decision-making process that considers everyone's input and aligns with your family's values and purpose.
- **What Challenges Might We Face?**: Recognize potential obstacles and challenges that may impact your family's well-being and progress.
- **Strategies to Overcome Challenges**: Develop plans to address and overcome these challenges, including action steps, required resources, and contingency measures.

## Our Family's Culture

- **What Makes Our Family Unique?**: Identify the aspects that make your family special and distinct from others.
- **How Will We Preserve Our Family's Heritage?**: Determine ways to maintain and pass down your family's traditions, values, and cultural heritage.
- **Is Everything Aligned?**: Ensure that all aspects of your family's vision, values, and actions are coherent and support each other.
