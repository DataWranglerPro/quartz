### **Phase 1: Identification (The Lists)**

**1. Handout: "[[LIST OF DEMONIC STRONGHOLDS|List of Demonic Strongholds]]"** (Section 5.3)
*   **What to do:** Go through this list and **circle** all items that apply to your life, whether past or present. Use this as a guideline to identify areas where you feel stuck or oppressed.
*   **Special Instruction:** Section 3.2 notes that while the minister will pray for everything on the list, they will focus more heavily on the areas you have circled.
*   **Disposition:** **BRING THIS TO THE SESSION.**

**2. Handout: "[[Open Doors|Demonic Strongholds Entry Points]]"** (Section 5.4)
*   **What to do:** This sheet lists 9 categories (Generational Curses, Unforgiveness, Occult, Sexual Sin, Soul Ties, Covenants, Pride, Idolatry, Unconfessed Sin). You must seek God and write down **specific names, events, and details** for each category.

*   **Disposition:** **DO NOT BRING THIS TO THE SESSION.** You will destroy this list after you finish your declarations (see Phase 2).

### **Phase 2: Action (Renunciation & Declarations)**

**3. Handout: "[[Closing Doors|Closing the Door Declarations]]"** (Section 5.6)
*   **What to do:** You must read these proclamations **out loud**. They correspond to the categories in your "Entry Points" list.
*   **How to use it:** When the declaration asks you to "list" or "name" something (e.g., "I now break and sever every ungodly soul ties with..."), you read the specific names you wrote down on your private "Entry Points" list.
*   **Timing:** Section 5.5 instructs you to make these declarations out loud **the night or day before** the deliverance session.
*   **Disposition:** Keep at home.

**4. Handout: "Freemasonry Prayer of Release"** (Section 5.11)
*   **What to do:** *If applicable* (if you or your ancestors were involved in Freemasonry), you should pray through this specific release prayer to renounce those oaths.
*   **Disposition:** Keep at home.

### **Phase 3: Clean Up (Physical & Spiritual)**

**5. Handout: "Home Cleansing Prayer & Blessing"** (Section 5.12) & **"Preparing For Deliverance"** (Section 5.5)
*   **What to do:** Perform a "house cleaning in the natural realm." You are to remove/destroy objects associated with the occult, idolatry, or sin.
    *   *Items to destroy:* Idols, occult games (Ouija, D&D), pornography, New Age materials, drug paraphernalia, and objects associated with false religions or superstitions.
*   **Disposition:** Destroy/Burn these items at home.

**6. Final Step: Destroying the "Entry Points" List**
*   **What to do:** Once you have made your verbal declarations (Step 3) and renounced the specific sins/names listed on your "Entry Points" sheet, Section 5.5 explicitly commands: **"Burn the 'Entry Points' List when you are done."**
*   **Why:** This removes the record of sins that have now been confessed and covered by the blood of Jesus.
