
# **D&M DELIVERANCE**

**Document Classification:** Internal Ministry SOP  
**Use Case:** Individual or Group Deliverance  
**Pace:** Accelerated / Same-Session Deliverance

---

## **MINISTER SCRIPT – OPENING ORIENTATION (REQUIRED)**

> “Before we begin, I want to explain how this will work so you feel safe and informed.
> 
> I will ask simple yes-or-no questions to identify major strongholds.
> 
> If you answer yes, you will renounce that area, and I will immediately take authority and command the spirit to leave.
> 
> You do not need to explain, relive trauma, or remember everything.
> 
> Your role is simply to agree and cooperate.
> 
> My role is to carry the authority and lead the process.”

---

# **PHASE 1: THE GATEKEEPER**

**Principle:**  
Deliverance requires submission to Jesus Christ and willingness to relinquish legal rights.

---
### **Minister Explanation (Say This):**

> “Deliverance only works when Jesus is recognized as Lord and when you genuinely want freedom. These questions simply confirm that.”

---

### **Required Questions**

1. **Salvation Check**
    
    > “Have you already given your life to Jesus, or do you want to do that right now?”
    
2. **Readiness Check**
    
    > “Are you ready to be free and kick these spirits out for good?”
    

✔ Submission to authority  
✔ Voluntary participation  
✔ No forced deliverance

If either answer is **No** → stop.

---

# **PHASE 2: LEGAL SETUP**

### _(Legal Rights, Covering, Forgiveness)_

**Time:** ~5 minutes

---
### **Minister Explanation**

> “Before we address any strongholds, we must remove legal barriers.  
> This step establishes spiritual authority and removes unforgiveness, which will block deliverance.”

---
### **2.1 Opening Prayer (Authority & Covering)**

**Minister:**

> “Lord, we ask that Your Holy Spirit fill this room.  
> We cover this time, this place, and this person under the Blood of Jesus Christ.”

✔ Authority invoked  
✔ Spiritual covering established

---

### **2.2 Umbrella Forgiveness Declaration**

**Minister Explanation**

> “Forgiveness removes legal rights from the enemy.  
> You do not need to name anyone—this prayer covers all offenses.”

**Client Repeats:**

> “Lord, I choose to forgive everyone who has hurt me.  
> I release them.  
> I forgive myself.  
> I place all these hurts under the Blood of Jesus.”

✔ Legal rights removed  
✔ No trauma excavation  
✔ Forgiveness completed


---

# **PHASE 3: ASK & CAST LOOP (CORE STRONGHOLD REMOVAL)**

**Time:** 30–45 minutes

---

### **Minister Explanation (Critical):**

> “Now we will address the main strongholds.  
> I will ask a simple question for each one.  
> If the answer is yes, you will renounce it, and I will immediately command it to leave.
> 
> You do not need to describe anything or relive it.  
> We are removing legal rights and enforcing Jesus’ authority.”

---

## **STRONGMAN 1: OCCULT / FALSE RELIGION**

_(Idolatry / Divination)_

**Ask:**

> “Have you ever participated in the occult, horoscopes, false religions, or Freemasonry?”

**Client:**

> “I renounce the Occult and all false gods.”

**Minister:**

> “Spirit of Divination and Occult, I bind you.  
> I break your legal rights over this person and this bloodline.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

## **STRONGMAN 2: PERVERSION / UNGODLY SOUL TIES**

_(Sexual Sin & Trauma)_

**Ask:**

> “Have you participated in sexual sin outside of marriage, or were you a victim of sexual abuse?”

**Client:**

> “I renounce sexual sin and break all ungodly soul ties.”

**Minister:**

> “Spirit of Perversion and Lust, your legal rights are revoked.  
> You cannot stay.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

## **STRONGMAN 3: REJECTION / BITTERNESS**

_(Rejection, Heaviness)_

**Ask:**

> “Have you experienced deep rejection, abuse, or self-hatred?”

**Client:**

> “I renounce Rejection and Self-Hate.”

**Minister:**

> “Strongman of Rejection and Heaviness, release this mind and emotions.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

## **STRONGMAN 4: FEAR / TRAUMA RESPONSE**

_(Fear, Control)_

**Ask:**

> “Do you struggle with anxiety, panic, or needing control to feel safe?”

**Client:**

> “I renounce the Spirit of Fear.”

**Minister:**

> “Spirit of Fear and Trauma, perfect love casts you out.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

## **STRONGMAN 5: PRIDE / REBELLION**

_(Pride, Rebellion, Anger)_

**Ask:**

> “Do you struggle with anger, rebellion, or resisting authority?”

**Client:**

> “I renounce Pride and Rebellion.”

**Minister:**

> “Spirit of Pride and Haughtiness, bow to the authority of Jesus Christ.  
> **GO now**.”

---

## **STRONGMAN 6: ADDICTION / BONDAGE**

_(Bondage, Pharmakeia)_

**Ask:**

> “Are you bound by an addiction you cannot stop?”

**Client:**

> “I renounce this addiction.”

**Minister:**

> “Spirit of Bondage and Addiction, your chains are broken.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

# **PHASE 4: FILLING & SEALING**

_(Post-Deliverance Care)_  
**Time:** ~5 minutes

---

### **Minister Explanation**

> “Deliverance always ends with filling.  
> Where something leaves, the Holy Spirit fills.”

---

### **Impartation Prayer**

**Minister:**

> “Lord, the house is clean.  
> Fill [Name] with Your Holy Spirit.  
> Replace fear with peace, rejection with love, and bondage with rest.”

---

### **Digital Aftercare Handoff**

**Minister:**

> “You are free.  
> Watch this short teaching tonight to learn how to keep your door closed.”

---

# **CLOSING SUMMARY (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)**

> “What we did today:  
> we removed legal rights, renounced strongholds, and exercised authority.  
> Your responsibility now is to stand in what God has done.”
