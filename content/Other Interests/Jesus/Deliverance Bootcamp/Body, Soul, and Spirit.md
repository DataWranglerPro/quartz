Using the **NIV**, here is an explanation of how a person is composed of a Body, a Soul, and a Spirit.

Think of a human being like a **House**.
1. **The Body** is the structure (wood, bricks, paint).
2. **The Soul** is the life happening inside (the people, the conversation, the atmosphere).
3. **The Spirit** is the electricity/power that connects the house to the grid (God).

### Part 1: The 3 Parts of You (The Blueprint)

The Bible clearly shows we are a "trinity" of sorts—three parts making up one person. The clearest verse for this is **1 Thessalonians 5:23**:

> "May God himself, the God of peace, sanctify you through and through. May your whole **spirit, soul and body** be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ."

#### 1. The Body (The Exterior)

This is your "Earth suit." It interacts with the physical world through the five senses (touch, taste, sight, smell, hearing).

- **Genesis 2:7:** "Then the Lord God formed a man from the **dust of the ground** and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living being."
- **2 Corinthians 5:1:** "For we know that if the earthly **tent** we live in is destroyed, we have a building from God, an eternal house in heaven, not built by human hands."
    - (The Bible calls the body a "tent"—it's just a temporary place where the real "you" lives).

#### 2. The Soul (The Interior Life)

This is your personality. It includes your mind (thoughts), emotions (feelings), and will (decisions). It is the part of you that makes you, you.

- **Psalm 13:2:** "How long must I wrestle with my **thoughts** and day after day have sorrow in my **heart**?"
    - (This shows the soul struggles with thinking and feeling).
- **Matthew 26:38:** Jesus said, "My **soul is overwhelmed with sorrow** to the point of death."

#### 3. The Spirit (The Power Source)

This is the deepest part of you. It is designed to "plug in" to God. Without God, the spirit is "dead" (disconnected), but when you become a Christian, the Holy Spirit plugs you back in.

- **Proverbs 20:27:** "The human **spirit is the lamp of the Lord** that sheds light on one’s inmost being."
- **Romans 8:16:** "The Spirit (God's Spirit) himself testifies with **our spirit** that we are God’s children."

### The Soul vs the Spirit

* **Hebrews 4:12:** "For the word of God is alive and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword, it penetrates even to dividing soul and spirit, joints and marrow; it judges the thoughts and attitudes of the heart."

**Explanation of the Metaphor**

* "**Joints and Marrow**": In the physical body, joints (where bones connect) and marrow (the substance deep inside the bone) are intimately linked. You cannot cleanly "divide" the marrow from the bone or the joint without destroying the structure. They are biologically intertwined.
* "**Soul and Spirit**": Similarly, the "soul" (your mind, emotions, and personality) and the "spirit" (the part of you that connects to God) are so tightly woven together that human beings often cannot tell them apart. We often mistake emotional experiences (soul) for spiritual ones (spirit).
* **The Meaning:** The metaphor means that only the Word of God is sharp enough to cut through that confusion. It penetrates deep enough to show you what is merely you (your feelings, thoughts, ego) and what is truly from God (spiritual conviction). It exposes your true motives.

### Part 2: Where Can Demons Mess with You?

If you are a Christian, imagine your life is a castle owned by God.

![[Pasted image 20251124154545.png]]

**The Spirit is the "King's room."**  
This is the secure heart of the castle where the King resides. The drawbridge is up and the door is locked. No enemy weapon can penetrate this stronghold.

**The Soul is the "Courtyard."**  
This is the area inside the walls but outside the King's room. The enemy can shoot flaming arrows over the wall into the courtyard, causing chaos, fear, and smoke in your mind and emotions.

**The Body is the "Outer Wall."**  
The enemy can batter the walls and chip the stone (physical affliction), but he cannot claim the throne inside the Keep.

Here is the Bible evidence for where they can strike and where they cannot.

#### 1. Can they affect the BODY? (YES)

The Bible shows clearly that demons can cause physical problems. This doesn't mean every sickness is a demon, but they do have the power to hurt the physical body.

- **The "Thorn" (Pain):** Paul was filled with the Holy Spirit, but he was physically attacked.
    - **2 Corinthians 12:7:** "I was given a **thorn in my flesh**, a **messenger of Satan**, to torment me."
- **Disability:** Jesus healed a woman who was bent over and couldn't stand up.
    - **Luke 13:11:** "...a woman was there who had been **crippled by a spirit** for eighteen years."
- **Sensory issues:**
    - **Matthew 9:32-33:** "...a man who was **demon-possessed and could not talk** was brought to Jesus." (When the demon left, he was healed).

#### 2. Can they affect the SOUL/MIND? (YES)

This is their favorite target. They can't steal your soul, but they can try to cause havoc with fear, lies, and confusion.

- **Blinding the Mind (Thoughts):** They try to stop you from understanding truth.
    - **2 Corinthians 4:4:** "The god of this age (Satan) has **blinded the minds** of unbelievers..."
- **Fear (Emotions):** They project feelings of terror.
    - **2 Timothy 1:7:** "For the Spirit God gave us does not make us timid (fearful)..."
        - (This implies that the spirit of fear comes from the enemy, not God).
- **Planting Ideas (The Will):**  
    * **John 13:2:** "The evening meal was in progress, and the devil had already prompted Judas... to betray Jesus."  
    * (The devil put a suggestion into Judas's mind/heart).

#### 3. Can they affect the SPIRIT? (NO - If you are a believer)

This is the crucial difference. If you have accepted Jesus, your spirit is "sealed." It is like a safe that only God has the combination to.

- **The Seal:** You are marked as God's private property.
    - **Ephesians 1:13:** "...When you believed, you were **marked in him with a seal**, the promised Holy Spirit."
- **The Occupant:** A demon cannot live in the same room as the Holy Spirit.
    - **1 Corinthians 6:19:** "Do you not know that your bodies are **temples of the Holy Spirit**, who is in you...?"
    - **1 John 4:4:** "the One who is in you is **greater** than the one who is in the world."
- **The Protection:**
    - **1 John 5:18:** "We know that anyone born of God (in their spirit) does not continue to sin; the One who was born of God keeps them safe, and **the evil one cannot harm them**."

**The "Straight Talk" (No Verse, just logic):**  
The Bible never explicitly says "Demons cannot touch the spirit," but it does say that Light and Darkness cannot exist in the same place (2 Corinthians 6:14). Since the Holy Spirit literally lives inside you, a demon cannot enter that space without overpowering God, which is impossible.

**Summary:**
- **Body:** Demons can attack the outer wall (Sickness/Pain).
- **Soul:** Demons can shoot flaming arrows over the wall (Anxiety/Temptation).
- **Spirit:** **OFF LIMITS.**
