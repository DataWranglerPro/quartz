#### 1. The Theological Framework
Explaining **where** the demons reside.
*   **Concept:** Man is a Trinity (Body, Soul, Spirit).
*   **Logic:** When a person is saved, their **Spirit** is renewed. However, the **Soul** (Mind, Will, Emotions) and **Body** can still harbor demonic oppression ("rats in the house, but they don't own the house").
*   **Result:** You don't "re-save" the person; you clean out the soul.

#### 2. The 5-Session Process
This is the operational "How-To" of the Deliverance process.
1.  **Investigation:** Find out where the trauma/sin is (Interview).
2.  **Education:** Teach the client *how* the enemy got in (Entry Points).
3.  **Legal Work:** The client must "cancel the contract" (Renunciation/Confession) before the spirits are kicked out. *Note: The client must do this work themselves.*
4.  **Eviction:** The actual prayer session where the minister takes authority.
5.  **Rehabilitation:** Teaching the client how to stop the spirits from returning.
