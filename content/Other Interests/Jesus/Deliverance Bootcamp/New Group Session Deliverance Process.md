
**Ideal Group Size:**  
20–150 (larger requires trained prayer team support)

**Total Time:**  
75–90 minutes

**Core Rule:**

> **The group receives deliverance by agreement.  
> The minister carries authority.**

---

## **HIGH-LEVEL GROUP FLOW**

1. **Orientation & Safety Briefing (10 min)**
2. **Group Gatekeeper & Consent (5 min)**
3. **Legal Setup (Forgiveness & Covering) (10 min)**
4. **Strongman Deliverance by Category (30–40 min)**
5. **Holy Spirit Filling & Stabilization (10 min)**
6. **Clear Dismissal & Aftercare Direction (5 min)**

---

## **1️⃣ ORIENTATION & SAFETY BRIEFING (10 min)**

This step is **non-negotiable** for group work.

### **Minister Script (Say This Clearly):**

> Before we begin, I want to explain exactly how this session will work so you feel safe and informed.
> 
> This is not counseling, interrogation, or emotional processing.  
> You will not be asked to explain, defend, or relive past experiences.
> 
> I will ask you a series of simple yes-or-no questions to identify areas where the enemy may have gained access.  
> 
> If something applies to you, you will either agree silently or briefly repeat a short renunciation.
> 
> Once you give consent, I will take authority in Jesus’ name and command the stronghold to leave.  
> 
> You do not need to analyze, perform, or make anything happen.
> 
> You do not need to manifest, speak out, or react in any particular way for this to work.  
> Your role is simply to cooperate and receive.
> 
> If at any point you feel overwhelmed, let me know. We can pause, slow down, or stop at any time.  
> 
> You are in control of your participation throughout the entire session.”

### **Group Rules (State Explicitly):**

- No shouting
- No self-commanding
- No chasing experiences
- Eyes open or closed—your choice
- Stay seated unless assisted

---

## **2️⃣ GROUP GATEKEEPER & CONSENT (5 min)**

### **Minister Explanation:**

> “Deliverance works only with consent and submission to Jesus Christ.  
> If this does not apply to you, you may remain seated and simply observe.”

### **Group Questions (Hands NOT Raised):**

> “If Jesus is Lord of your life—or you are willing for Him to be right now—agree silently.”

> “If you want freedom and are ready to kick these spirits out for good—agree silently.”

✔ Voluntary consent  
✔ Authority recognized  
✔ No forced participation

---

## **3️⃣ LEGAL SETUP (GROUP FORGIVENESS & COVERING) (10 min)**

### **Minister Explanation:**

> “Before we remove strongholds, we must remove legal rights.  
> Unforgiveness is the most common blocker.”

---

### **Opening Prayer**

> “Holy Spirit, we ask You to fill this room.  
> We cover this entire gathering under the Blood of Jesus Christ.”

---

### **Group Umbrella Forgiveness**

**Minister:**

> “Picture anyone who has ever hurt you.”

**Group repeats softly or agrees silently:**

> “Lord, I choose to forgive everyone who has hurt me.  
> I release them.  
> I forgive myself.  
> I place all these hurts under the Blood of Jesus.”

No names. No delay.

---

## **4️⃣ STRONGMAN DELIVERANCE (GROUP ASK & CAST) (30–40 min)**

### **Minister Explanation (Critical for Order):**

> “Now we will remove strongholds one at a time.  
> When I ask the question, if it applies to you, agree quietly.
> 
> I will then command the strongman to leave the entire group.
> 
> Do not analyze. Do not resist. Simply receive.”

---

### **Group Strongman Pattern (Used for All 6)**

**Ask the Question → Pause 5–10 seconds → Command**

No verbal responses required unless instructed.

---

### **STRONGMAN SEQUENCE (GROUP FORMAT)**

#### **1. OCCULT / FALSE RELIGION**

> “If you have ever participated in the occult, false religion, or counterfeit spirituality—agree silently.”

**Command:**

> “Spirit of Divination and Occult, I bind you.  
> Break your power over this group and their bloodlines.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

#### **2. PERVERSION / UNGODLY SOUL TIES**

> “If sexual sin, abuse, or ungodly soul ties have been part of your story—agree silently.”

**Command:**

> “Spirit of Perversion and Lust, your legal rights are revoked.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

#### **3. REJECTION / SELF-HATE**

> “If rejection, abandonment, or self-hatred has marked your life—agree silently.”

**Command:**

> “Strongman of Rejection and Heaviness, release this group.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

#### **4. FEAR / TRAUMA**

> “If fear, anxiety, panic, or control has ruled your nervous system—agree silently.”

**Command:**

> “Spirit of Fear and Trauma, perfect love casts you out.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

#### **5. PRIDE / REBELLION**

> “If anger, rebellion, or resistance to authority has had influence—agree silently.”

**Command:**

> “Spirit of Pride and Haughtiness, bow to Jesus Christ.  
> **GO now**.”

---

#### **6. ADDICTION / BONDAGE**

> “If addiction or compulsive behavior has controlled you—agree silently.”

**Command:**

> “Spirit of Bondage and Addiction, your chains are broken.  
> **GO now** in Jesus’ name.”

---

## **5️⃣ HOLY SPIRIT FILLING & STABILIZATION (10 min)**

### **Minister Explanation:**

> “Where something leaves, the Holy Spirit fills.  
> There is no emptiness here.”

**Prayer:**

> “Holy Spirit, fill every person in this room.  
> Bring peace, safety, and rest.  
> Seal what You have done.”

---

## **6️⃣ DISMISSAL & AFTERCARE (5 min)**

### **Minister Instructions:**

> “You are free.  
> If a thought tries to return, do not analyze—resist it.  
> Stay connected to God and healthy people.
> 
> Scan the QR code or follow the link tonight to learn how to stay free.”

---

# **ROOM LOGISTICS & SAFETY CONTROLS**

✔ Trained prayer team positioned on edges  
✔ No microphones passed  
✔ No laying on of hands unless needed  
✔ Anyone overwhelmed is gently escorted out—not spotlighted

---

# **WHEN TO ESCALATE SOMEONE OUT OF GROUP**

Remove quietly if:

- Loud verbal manifestation
- Physical instability
- Severe dissociation
- Trauma flooding

These cases move to **private ministry**, not continued group deliverance.

---

## **WHY THIS GROUP MODEL WORKS**

• Fast-paced without chaos  
• Authority-centered  
• Participant-safe  
• Scales cleanly  
• No homework burden
