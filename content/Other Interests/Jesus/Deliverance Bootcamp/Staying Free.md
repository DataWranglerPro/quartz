Walking the Narrow Road (Matt 7:13-14)

DEMONIC SPIRITS WILL SEEK TO RETURN
1. Matthew 12:43-45. They are deceptive and will work in our minds and through
others to try to find an open door or crack in your armor.
2. Luke 4:13, When Satan tempted Jesus, Satan planned to return at a later time.
REQUIREMENTS FOR STAYING FREE
3. Yield every area of your life to the Lordship of Christ. Make Him first!
John 1:11-12; Matthew 7:21-23, John 12:31-32
4. Seek to be continually filled with the Holy Spirit. Ephesians 5:18; Romans 8:13
5. Live by the Word of God. Luke 11:13; Matthew 4:4, 7, 11; Ephesians 6:17
6. Utilize the whole armor of God by faith and word. Ephesians 6:10-18
7. Renew your mind daily (Bible meditation), so you may be transformed. Romans 12:1-2; II
Cor 10:3–5; Romans 8:5-7; Col. 3:1-2
8. Pray in the Spirit. Romans 8:26-27; I Cor. 14:2, 14-15; Jude 20
9. Practice praise & worship at all times. Isaiah 61:3; 60:18; Hebrew 13:15
10. Cultivate the right relationships. Matthew 18:15-20; I John 1:7; I John 2:9-11
11. Avoid those people who are a bad influence on your life. James 4:4
12. Actively participate in a "good" church.
13. Proclaim the Word of God. Hebrews 3:1, 4:14, 10:23
14. Seek to obey the Lord in every area of your life. When you sin, make an immediate
confession. Be quick to forgive. I John 1:9
Note: Conviction comes from the Holy Spirit. Condemnation comes from demons.
15. Daily wrestle down the flesh by submitting to God and resisting the demonic spirits. You
must discern the spirits! James 4:7; Romans 6:6,11
16. Stay humble. James 4:6
17. Use your authority in Jesus Christ. Matthew 16:19
DO NOT BE DISCOURAGED!
18. It can take up to six weeks to develop new habits!
19. Sometimes deliverance occurs in phases and stages. God knows what we can handle!
20. Deliverance needs to be "walked out." You will learn how to win the battle of the mind!
