# Is the Deliverance Ministry Relevant in 2025?

Jesus did not intend for the work of deliverance to end with Him; He explicitly transferred His authority to drive out demons to us. It began in **Matthew 10:1** when He commanded the disciples to `drive out demons.` But He expanded this scope in **Mark 16:17** and **John 14:12**, promising that all `who believe` would possess this same power to cast out spirits in His name. **Luke 10:19** confirms the extent of this gift, authority over `all the power of the enemy.` As we see in **Acts 16:18**, the early church used this power after Jesus ascended, proving that casting out demons is a current, active assignment for the believer.

A [[Statistical Analysis of the Church of Acts|statistical analysis of the church of Acts]] reveals that casting out of demons was still occurring after Jesus' ministry on earth ended.

* **1. The Leader: Satan**
	* **John 12:31:** He is the ruler of this world
	* **John 8:44:** He is a deceiver and the father of lies
	* **Ephesians 2:2:** He operates in the spiritual atmosphere ("the air")
* **2. The Hierarchy: An Organized Structure**
	* **Ephesians 6:12**
		-  **Rulers:** High-level commanders.
		-  **Authorities:** Those with the right to carry out orders.
		-  **Powers of this dark world:** Forces operating globally or over specific regions.

* **3. The Subjects: Demons (Fallen Angels)**
	* **Revelation 12:3-4:** Their origin (The Rebellion).
	* **James 2:19:** They acknowledge God's power (and fear Him).
	* **Mark 5:12:** They can inhabit people or animals.

# Can a Christian Have a Demon?

The Bible says [[Body, Soul, and Spirit|you are made of three parts]] (**1 Thessalonians 5:23**).

**1. The BODY**
**2. The SOUL**
**3. The SPIRIT**

If you are a Christian, imagine you are a castle. [[Where Can Demons Mess with You|Here is where the enemy can (and cannot) mess with you.]]

**The Body (The Walls) → VULNERABLE**
*   **Can the enemy attack here?** **YES.**
*   **How:** Sickness, pain, or physical exhaustion.
*   *Paul had a "thorn in the flesh" sent to torment him.*

**The Soul (The Courtyard) → VULNERABLE**
*   **Can the enemy attack here?** **YES.**
*   **How:** Fear, anxiety, bad thoughts, and confusion.
*   *The enemy shoots "flaming arrows" of lies into your mind.*

**The Spirit (The King's Room) → OFF LIMITS**
*   **Can the enemy attack here?** **NO.**
*   **Why:** If you are a believer, the Holy Spirit lives here.
*   **Ephesians 1:13:** Darkness cannot enter a room filled with Light. You are sealed by God.
