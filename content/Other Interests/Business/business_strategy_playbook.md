# Our Why
- **Values we abide by (Do's)**: Identify the core principles and behaviors that guide your business decisions and actions. These are the positive values you consistently uphold.
- **Why do we exist?**: Understand the fundamental reason behind your business’s existence. It’s about the deeper purpose that drives your company beyond just making a profit.
- **Why is it important?**: Explain the significance of your business’s purpose. This could include the impact on customers, community, industry, or even the environment.
- **Against our Values (Don'ts)**: Define the actions and behaviors that go against your business’s core values. These are the things you strive to avoid.
- **What will we work on to achieve this?**: Outline the specific goals and initiatives you will focus on to stay true to your values and achieve your purpose.

# Our Destination
- **What does success look like for me?**: Describe your personal vision of success. This is how you, as the business owner, define your own success.
    - **Why is this destination important?**: Reflect on why achieving this personal vision of success matters to you. This could be about personal fulfillment, lifestyle, or other individual reasons.
- **What does ultimate success look like for the business?**: Define the long-term vision and ultimate goals for your business. This encompasses what your business aims to achieve at its peak.
    - **Why is this destination important?**: Explain the importance of this business vision. This might include the benefits to customers, employees, and the overall market impact.

# How are we going to do this?
- **Where are we going to play?**: Determine the markets or segments in which your business will operate. This includes geographic locations, industry sectors, or customer demographics.
- **Who are we going to target?**: Identify your target audience or customer base. Specify the key characteristics of your ideal customers.
- **How are we going to help them?**: Explain the value your business provides to its customers. How will your products or services meet their needs or solve their problems?
- **What challenges do we have to overcome?**: Recognize the obstacles and challenges that might hinder your business's progress. This includes internal and external factors.
- **Strategic approaches to overcome challenges**: Develop strategies to address and overcome these challenges. This includes action plans, resources needed, and contingencies.

# Positioning
- **How will we stand out?**: Identify what makes your business unique and different from the competition. This is your unique selling proposition (USP).
- **How can we make it difficult for our competitors to copy?**: Determine the aspects of your business that can be protected or made difficult to replicate. This might include proprietary technology, unique processes, or strong branding.
- **Is everything aligned?**: Ensure that all aspects of your business strategy, operations, and values are coherent and support each other. Alignment ensures consistency and effectiveness in reaching your goals.
