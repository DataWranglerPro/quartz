
# Side Hustle (1 person)
The idea is to run a campaign that has a start date and an end date. The whole purpose of the campaign is for you to come up with an idea that will make you money.
- Do not create a campaign that takes more than 90 days
- There is no long term commitment
	- The idea is that you get to practice how to make money. 
	- If the idea fails, that is ok. You get to start a new campaign and try a different idea.

# Co-Founders (1-3 people)
This is the first team that includes people with complimentary skills. The goal of the scout team is to quickly and cheaply test business ideas.
- The typical team structure consist of two people
	- Sales/Marketing - Can we sale it?
	- Ops/Technical - Can we build it?
- Conduct fast and cheap experiments
	- Strategies to test the idea
		- A waiting list
		- Intro event/Webinar
		- Group
	- Is the idea Good?
		- Throw away the idea - If you cannot even get 30 persons to sign up
		- Minimum - 30 persons
		- Better - 150 persons
		- Very good - 1000+ persons
- At this stage
	- You are not setting up a company
	- You are not setting up a bank account
	- The whole point is to validate your business idea


# Fire Team (3-6 People)
You need a team with a shared vision, mission, and values.
- The typical team structure consist of four people
	- KPI - Key person of influence
		- This person opens doors
	- Sales
	- Ops/Technical - Customer fulfillment (ES score)
	- SAK - Swiss Army Knife
		- This person can do many things (not all great)
		- This person fills in the gaps
- Campaigns
	- LAPS - weekly
	- Experience Score (ES) - On a scale of 1-10, how happy is the customer
		- We want a score above an 8
	- Spot Light Campaign (SL) - Get all of the leads that did not convert and give them something exciting (gift or something to do). The idea is to re-engage them and run them through the LAPS process again.
